Case 01 · Content / Events / LiveOps

Atlantis Odyssey

I designed and shipped expedition content end to end — story, quests, maps and cutscenes — and kept it engaging through feature implementation, progression balancing and LiveOps events.

ExpeditionsQuest & Content DesignBalancingLiveOps
Game Designer · VIZOR · Aug 2022 – Feb 2025

My contribution

  • Expedition & Game Design: Independently designed and developed 15+ expeditions from concept to release, including gameplay mechanics, quest structure, narrative, maps, and cutscenes. Contributed to 40+ additional expeditions.
  • Content & Feature Integration: Integrated new locations into existing game features, worked with gameplay systems, and managed content implementation.
  • Content Production & Launch: Wrote in-game announcements, prepared art briefs, integrated assets, and launched new locations.
  • Balancing & LiveOps: Balanced player progression, configured live events, and mentored 5+ game designers.
Featured Design Decision · Late-Expedition Pacing

Players were running out of content before the expedition ended. To improve retention and extend the finale, I introduced more engaging mechanics, including multi-layered path blockers and complex crafting chains, alongside progressively harder quests. This kept players engaged longer and encouraged greater resource investment to complete the location.

Case 03 · Level Design / HOPA

Manor Matters & Mystery Matters

Designed and implemented hidden-object levels for two HOPA titles: object lists, placement and difficulty balancing.

Level Designer · VOKI · Feb 2021 – May 2022

My contribution

  • Level content: Built hidden-object levels, choosing objects and their placement within level-design guidelines.
  • Art collaboration: Wrote graphics requests and reviewed visual quality with artists.
  • Balancing: Tuned level difficulty and documented the results.
  • Ideas: Proposed new level mechanics and worked with programmers, animators, QA and production.
Design focus

Balancing object placement, scene composition and the clarity of hidden-object gameplay.

View process and design considerations
  1. Define the object list and placement according to level-design guidelines.
  2. Request and review artwork with artists, checking that objects stay readable within the scene.
  3. Balance difficulty through object placement and visual hierarchy.
  4. Test with QA and the team, then refine and document the level.
